# Exercise 14 — A Small Change in the Right-Hand Side

The first two equations imply \(3x=3\), hence \(x=1\), and then \(y=1\). The first system's third equation is \(3x=3\), so it is consistent, with unique solution \((1,1)\).

The modified third equation requires \(3x=3.1\), contradicting \(x=1\); the second system has no solution. The first two equations force the left-hand side \(3x\) to equal exactly \(3\), so changing only its required right-hand side makes the system inconsistent.
