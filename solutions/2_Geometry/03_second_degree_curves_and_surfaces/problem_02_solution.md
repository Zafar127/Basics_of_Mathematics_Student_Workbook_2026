# Exercise 2 — Circle by Completing the Square

## Question

Reduce \(x^2+y^2-6x+4y-3=0\) to canonical form, find the center and radius, and check two simple points in both forms.

## Solution

Complete the square in each variable:
\[
(x^2-6x)+(y^2+4y)=3,
\qquad (x-3)^2+(y+2)^2=16.
\]
Thus the circle has center \((3,-2)\) and radius \(4\). The points \((7,-2)\) and \((3,2)\) are on the circle. In canonical form, each gives \(4^2=16\). In the original left-hand side, they give \(49+4-42-8-3=0\) and \(9+4-18+8-3=0\), respectively.
