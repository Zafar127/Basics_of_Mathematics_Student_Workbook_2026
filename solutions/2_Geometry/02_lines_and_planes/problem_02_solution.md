# Exercise 2 — A Line in Space

## Question

For \(L:(x,y,z)=(2,-1,3)+t(1,2,-2)\), state a point and direction vector, test whether \(P=(4,3,-1)\) lies on \(L\), and find the point for \(t=-1\).

## Solution

The line passes through \((2,-1,3)\) and has direction vector \((1,2,-2)\). To reach \(P\), the first coordinate requires \(t=2\), but this gives \(y=-1+2(2)=3\) and \(z=3-2(2)=-1\); hence \(P\) lies on \(L\). At \(t=-1\),
\[
(x,y,z)=(2,-1,3)-(1,2,-2)=(1,-3,5).
\]
