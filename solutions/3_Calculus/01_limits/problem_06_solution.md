# Exercise 6 — Limit at Infinity

## Solution

Divide numerator and denominator by \(x^2\):
\[
\frac{3x^2-x+1}{5x^2+4x-2}
=\frac{3-\frac1x+\frac1{x^2}}{5+\frac4x-\frac2{x^2}}
\longrightarrow\frac35.
\]
This is the same dominant-degree calculation as for a sequence: all lower-order terms vanish after normalization by the highest power. The variable is now real and approaches infinity continuously rather than taking only integer values.
