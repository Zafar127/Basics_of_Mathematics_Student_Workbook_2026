# Problem 1 — A Cubic Polynomial

## Solution

\[
f'(x)=3x^2-6x=3x(x-2).
\]
The function increases on \((-\infty,0)\), decreases on \((0,2)\), and increases on \((2,\infty)\). Thus \((0,2)\) is a local maximum and \((2,-2)\) is a local minimum. The cubic tends to \(-\infty\) as \(x\to-\infty\) and \(+\infty\) as \(x\to+\infty\); these facts and the critical points determine the overall sketch.
