# Exercise 10 — Triangle Area from Vectors

## Question

For \(A=(0,0,0)\), \(B=(2,1,0)\), and \(C=(1,3,2)\), construct \(\overrightarrow{AB}\) and \(\overrightarrow{AC}\), compute the parallelogram area using their cross product, and find the triangle area. Explain the factor \(\frac12\).

## Solution

\[
\overrightarrow{AB}=(2,1,0),\qquad \overrightarrow{AC}=(1,3,2).
\]
Their cross product is
\[
\overrightarrow{AB}\times\overrightarrow{AC}
=(2,-4,5),
\]
so the parallelogram area is
\[
\|\overrightarrow{AB}\times\overrightarrow{AC}\|
=\sqrt{2^2+(-4)^2+5^2}=\sqrt{45}=3\sqrt5.
\]
The triangle area is half the parallelogram area:
\[
\text{Area}_{\triangle ABC}=\frac12(3\sqrt5)=\frac{3\sqrt5}{2},
\]
because the diagonal of a parallelogram divides it into two congruent triangles.
