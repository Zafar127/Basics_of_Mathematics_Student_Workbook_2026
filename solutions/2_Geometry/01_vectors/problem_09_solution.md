# Exercise 9 — Cross Product as a Perpendicular Direction

## Question

For \(u=(1,2,0)\) and \(v=(0,1,3)\), compute \(u\times v\). Use dot products to check perpendicularity to both vectors, compute \(v\times u\), and compare the results.

## Solution

\[
u\times v=
\bigl(2\cdot3-0\cdot1,\ 0\cdot0-1\cdot3,\ 1\cdot1-2\cdot0\bigr)
=(6,-3,1).
\]
The dot products verify perpendicularity:
\[
(u\times v)\cdot u=6-6+0=0,\qquad
(u\times v)\cdot v=0-3+3=0.
\]
Reversing the order changes the sign:
\[
v\times u=(-6,3,-1)=-(u\times v).
\]
Both cross products point perpendicular to the same plane, but in opposite orientations.
