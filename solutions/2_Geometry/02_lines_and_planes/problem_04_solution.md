# Exercise 4 — Plane Through Three Points

## Question

Find the plane through \(A=(1,0,0)\), \(B=(0,1,0)\), and \(C=(0,0,1)\). Construct two vectors in the plane, use their cross product, and check the equation on all three points.

## Solution

Two vectors in the plane are
\[
\overrightarrow{AB}=(-1,1,0),\qquad \overrightarrow{AC}=(-1,0,1).
\]
Their cross product is a normal:
\[
n=\overrightarrow{AB}\times\overrightarrow{AC}=(1,1,1).
\]
Using point \(A\), the plane is
\[
(1,1,1)\cdot((x,y,z)-(1,0,0))=0,
\quad\text{or}\quad x+y+z=1.
\]
The points satisfy the equation: \(1+0+0=1\), \(0+1+0=1\), and \(0+0+1=1\).
