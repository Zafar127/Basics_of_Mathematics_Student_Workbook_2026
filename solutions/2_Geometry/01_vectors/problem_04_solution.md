# Exercise 4 — Dot Product and Perpendicularity

## Question

For \(u=(1,2,-1)\) and \(v=(2,-1,3)\), compute \(u\cdot v\) and determine whether they are perpendicular. Find \(a\) so that \(w=(2,-1,a)\) is perpendicular to \(u\), and check the result.

## Solution

\[
u\cdot v=1\cdot2+2\cdot(-1)+(-1)\cdot3=2-2-3=-3.
\]
Since the dot product is not zero, \(u\) and \(v\) are not perpendicular. For \(w\perp u\), require
\[
u\cdot w=1\cdot2+2\cdot(-1)+(-1)a=0,
\]
so \(-a=0\) and \(a=0\). The check is \(u\cdot(2,-1,0)=2-2+0=0\).
