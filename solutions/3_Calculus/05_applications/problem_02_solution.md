# Problem 2 — Maximum Area of a Rectangle

## Solution

Let one side be \(x\); the other is \(20-x\), since \(2x+2y=40\). Positive side lengths require \(0<x<20\), and
\[
A(x)=x(20-x)=20x-x^2.
\]
Then \(A'(x)=20-2x=0\) at \(x=10\), and \(A''(x)=-2<0\), proving a maximum. The dimensions are \(10\) by \(10\), with area \(100\).
