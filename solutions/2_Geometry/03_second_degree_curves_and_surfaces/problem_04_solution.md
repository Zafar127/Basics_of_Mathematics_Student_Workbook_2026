# Exercise 4 — Parabola

## Question

For \(y^2=12x\), identify the vertex, axis and opening direction. Find points for \(x=3\) and describe a sketch.

## Solution

The equation has form \(y^2=4px\), with \(4p=12\), so the vertex is \((0,0)\), the axis is the \(x\)-axis, and the parabola opens to the right. At \(x=3\), \(y^2=36\), so the points are \((3,6)\) and \((3,-6)\). Sketch a right-opening curve symmetric about the \(x\)-axis through these points and the vertex.
