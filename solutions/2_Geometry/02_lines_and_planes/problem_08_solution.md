# Exercise 8 — Intersection Point of a Line and a Plane

## Question

Find the intersection of \((x,y,z)=(1,0,2)+t(1,2,-1)\) with \(x+y+z=4\), and check it in both descriptions.

## Solution

Substitute \(x=1+t,\ y=2t,\ z=2-t\) into the plane:
\[
(1+t)+2t+(2-t)=4\quad\Longrightarrow\quad 3+2t=4,
\]
so \(t=\frac12\). The intersection point is
\[
\left(\frac32,1,\frac32\right).
\]
It is on the line at \(t=\frac12\), and its coordinate sum is \(\frac32+1+\frac32=4\), so it is on the plane.
