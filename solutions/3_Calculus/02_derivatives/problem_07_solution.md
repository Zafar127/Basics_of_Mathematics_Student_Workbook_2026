# Problem 7 — A Trigonometric Composition

## Question

For
$$f(x)=\sin(x^2+1)$$
compute the derivative. Explicitly identify the outer function, the inner function, and the derivative of the inner function. Finally, determine the sign of $f'(0)$.

## Solution

The outer function is \(\sin u\), the inner function is \(u=x^2+1\), and \(u'=2x\). Thus
\[
f'(x)=2x\cos(x^2+1).
\]
In particular \(f'(0)=0\), so its sign at zero is zero (the tangent is horizontal there).
