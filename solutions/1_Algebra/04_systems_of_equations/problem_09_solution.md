# Exercise 9 — A System from a Sum Condition

## Question

Find numbers $x,y$ satisfying
$$x+y=10,\qquad 2x+5y=32.$$
Solve the system and explain geometrically why the solution is unique.

## Solution

Subtract twice the first equation from the second:
\[
(2x+5y)-2(x+y)=32-20\quad\Longrightarrow\quad3y=12,
\]
so \(y=4\) and \(x=6\). The lines intersect uniquely because their normal vectors \((1,1)\) and \((2,5)\) are not proportional, so the lines are not parallel (their determinant is \(5-2=3\ne0\)).
