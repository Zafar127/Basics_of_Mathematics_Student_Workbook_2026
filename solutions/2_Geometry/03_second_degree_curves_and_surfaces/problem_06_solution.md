# Exercise 6 — Shifted Ellipse

## Question

Reduce \(4x^2+9y^2-16x+18y-11=0\) to canonical form, give the center and semiaxis lengths, and check the left-hand side at the center.

## Solution

Group and complete squares:
\[
4(x^2-4x)+9(y^2+2y)=11,
\]
\[
4\bigl((x-2)^2-4\bigr)+9\bigl((y+1)^2-1\bigr)=11,
\]
so
\[
4(x-2)^2+9(y+1)^2=36,
\qquad
\frac{(x-2)^2}{9}+\frac{(y+1)^2}{4}=1.
\]
The center is \((2,-1)\), and the semiaxis lengths are \(3\) horizontally and \(2\) vertically. At the center, the original left-hand side is \(16+9-32-18-11=-36\), matching the canonical equation's left side \(0\) minus \(36\).
