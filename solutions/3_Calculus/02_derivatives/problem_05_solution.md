# Problem 5 — The Chain Rule

## Solution

Let \(u(x)=3x^2-2x+1\) be the inner function and \(g(u)=u^5\) the outer function. Then \(u'(x)=6x-2\), \(g'(u)=5u^4\), and
\[
f'(x)=5(3x^2-2x+1)^4(6x-2).
\]
The factor \(6x-2\) is the derivative of the inner expression and accounts for how quickly its input changes with \(x\).
