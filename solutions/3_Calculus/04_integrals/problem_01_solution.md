# Problem 1 — Antiderivative and Verification

## Question

For
$$f(x)=3x^2-4x+5$$
find one antiderivative $F(x)$. Write the entire family of antiderivatives, verify the result by differentiation, and explain the meaning of the constant $C$.

## Solution

One antiderivative is \(F(x)=x^3-2x^2+5x\). The entire family is
\[
F(x)=x^3-2x^2+5x+C.
\]
Differentiating gives \(F'(x)=3x^2-4x+5\). The constant \(C\) is arbitrary because differentiating any constant gives zero; it represents the vertical shift among all antiderivatives.
