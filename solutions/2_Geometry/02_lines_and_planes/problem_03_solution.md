# Exercise 3 — Plane Through a Point with a Given Normal Vector

## Question

Find the plane through \(P=(1,-2,3)\) perpendicular to \(n=(2,1,-1)\). Check \(P\) and explain the role of \(n\).

## Solution

A plane perpendicular to \(n\) has \(n\) as a normal vector. Thus its equation is
\[
2(x-1)+(y+2)-(z-3)=0,
\]
or
\[
2x+y-z+3=0.
\]
Substituting \(P\) gives \(2-2-3+3=0\). The normal vector is perpendicular to every direction lying in the plane and determines its orientation.
