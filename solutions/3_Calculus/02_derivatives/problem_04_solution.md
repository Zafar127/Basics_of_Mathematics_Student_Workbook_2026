# Problem 4 — A Quotient and Domain Control

## Solution

The denominator cannot vanish, so the domain is \(\mathbb R\setminus\{1\}\). By the quotient rule,
\[
f'(x)=\frac{2x(x-1)-(x^2+1)}{(x-1)^2}
=\frac{x^2-2x-1}{(x-1)^2}.
\]
The restriction \(x\ne1\) remains: the original function and its derivative are undefined there, even though no factor cancels.
