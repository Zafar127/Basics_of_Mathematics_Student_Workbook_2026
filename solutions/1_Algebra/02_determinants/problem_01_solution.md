# Exercise 1 — Determinants of \(2\times2\) Matrices

## Question

Compute
$$\det\begin{pmatrix}3&-2\\5&4\end{pmatrix},\qquad \det\begin{pmatrix}1&7\\2&14\end{pmatrix}.$$
Which matrix is invertible? Justify your answer.

## Solution

\[
\det\begin{pmatrix}3&-2\\5&4\end{pmatrix}=3(4)-(-2)(5)=22,
\qquad
\det\begin{pmatrix}1&7\\2&14\end{pmatrix}=14-14=0.
\]

A square matrix is invertible exactly when its determinant is nonzero. Thus the first is invertible and the second is not.
