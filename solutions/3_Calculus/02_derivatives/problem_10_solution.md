# Problem 10 — The Tangent Line

## Question

For
$$f(x)=x^2-3x+1$$
find the equation of the tangent line at the point with $x=2$. Give the point of tangency, the value $f'(2)$, the equation of the line, and verify that the point lies on the obtained tangent line.

## Solution

For \(f(x)=x^2-3x+1\), \(f'(x)=2x-3\), so \(f'(2)=1\). The point of tangency is \((2,f(2))=(2,-1)\). The tangent equation is
\[
y+1=1(x-2),\qquad\text{or}\qquad y=x-3.
\]
At \(x=2\), this line gives \(y=-1\), confirming it passes through the point.
