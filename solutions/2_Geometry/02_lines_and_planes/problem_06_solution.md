# Exercise 6 — Angle Between Planes

## Question

For \(\Pi_1:x+y+z=1\) and \(\Pi_2:x-y=2\), use the normal vectors to determine whether the planes are parallel. If not, compute their angle and explain why the normals suffice.

## Solution

The normal vectors are \(n_1=(1,1,1)\) and \(n_2=(1,-1,0)\). They are not scalar multiples, so the planes are not parallel. Their dot product is zero:
\[
n_1\cdot n_2=1-1+0=0.
\]
Thus the normals are perpendicular, and the angle between the planes (the acute angle between their normals) is \(90^\circ\). Plane orientation is completely determined by its normal, which is why the normals suffice.
