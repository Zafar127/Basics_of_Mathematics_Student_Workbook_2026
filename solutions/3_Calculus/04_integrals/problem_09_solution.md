# Problem 9 — Definite Integral and Geometric Area

## Solution

\[
\int_{-1}^{2}x\,dx=\left[\frac{x^2}{2}\right]_{-1}^{2}
=2-\frac12=\frac32.
\]
The graph is below the axis on \([-1,0]\), giving geometric area \(1/2\), and above it on \([0,2]\), giving area \(2\). Total geometric area is \(5/2\), while the definite integral is signed: the below-axis part is subtracted.
