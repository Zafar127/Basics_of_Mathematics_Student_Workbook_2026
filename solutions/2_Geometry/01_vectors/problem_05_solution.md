# Exercise 5 — Angle Between Vectors

## Question

For \(u=(1,1,0)\) and \(v=(1,0,1)\), use the sign of \(u\cdot v\) to predict the type of angle, compute the angle, and compare it with the prediction.

## Solution

\[
u\cdot v=1,\qquad \|u\|=\sqrt2,\qquad \|v\|=\sqrt2.
\]
The positive dot product predicts an acute angle. Using \(u\cdot v=\|u\|\|v\|\cos\theta\),
\[
\cos\theta=\frac{1}{\sqrt2\sqrt2}=\frac12,
\qquad \theta=\arccos\left(\frac12\right)=\frac{\pi}{3}=60^\circ.
\]
The computed angle is acute, as predicted.
