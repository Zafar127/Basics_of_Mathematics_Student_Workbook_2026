# Exercise 2 — Different Growth Rates

## Solution

\[
\frac{3n+1}{n^2+4}
=\frac{\frac3n+\frac1{n^2}}{1+\frac4{n^2}}\longrightarrow 0.
\]
For the second quotient,
\[
\frac{2n^3-n}{5n^2+1}
=n\frac{2-\frac1{n^2}}{5+\frac1{n^2}}\longrightarrow+\infty.
\]
In general, for nonzero leading coefficients and positive \(n\), a rational function of polynomials tends to \(0\) when the numerator degree is smaller; when degrees agree, its limit is the ratio of leading coefficients; when the numerator degree is larger, its magnitude grows without bound (with sign determined by leading terms).
