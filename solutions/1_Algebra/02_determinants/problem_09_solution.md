# Exercise 9 — Area and the Determinant

Taking \(u,v\) as the columns,
\[
\det\begin{pmatrix}3&1\\1&4\end{pmatrix}=12-1=11.
\]
The parallelogram area is \(|11|=11\) square units. Reversing the columns gives determinant \(-11\); the sign changes with orientation, but the absolute value (area) stays \(11\).
