# Problem 1 — Derivative of a Polynomial

## Question

For
$$f(x)=3x^5-2x^3+7x-4$$
find $f'(x)$. Then compute $f'(0)$ and $f'(1)$, determine the signs of the obtained values, and explain what they say about the slope of the tangent line to the graph at these two points.

## Solution

By the power rule,
\[
f'(x)=15x^4-6x^2+7.
\]
Thus \(f'(0)=7>0\) and \(f'(1)=15-6+7=16>0\). At both points the tangent slopes are positive, so each tangent rises from left to right.
