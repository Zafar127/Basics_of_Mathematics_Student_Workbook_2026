# Exercise 7 — Linear Dependence Without Heavy Computation

## Question

Given \(u=(1,2,3)\), \(v=(2,4,6)\), and \(w=(0,1,1)\), identify a relation between \(u\) and \(v\), decide whether the three vectors can be linearly independent, and explain why no long calculation is needed.

## Solution

The second vector is twice the first: \(v=2u\), or \(v-2u=0\). Therefore the three vectors are linearly dependent: the nontrivial linear combination \(-2u+v+0w=0\) already proves dependence. No determinant or elimination is needed because the dependence is visible directly.
