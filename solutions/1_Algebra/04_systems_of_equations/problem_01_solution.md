# Exercise 1 — From a System to a Matrix

\[
A=\begin{pmatrix}2&-1&1\\1&3&-2\\3&1&1\end{pmatrix},\qquad
\mathbf{x}=\begin{pmatrix}x\\y\\z\end{pmatrix},\qquad
\mathbf{b}=\begin{pmatrix}4\\1\\7\end{pmatrix},
\quad A\mathbf{x}=\mathbf{b}.
\]

Each row of \(A\) contains the coefficients of \(x,y,z\) in the corresponding equation; the matching entry of \(\mathbf b\) is that equation's right-hand side.
