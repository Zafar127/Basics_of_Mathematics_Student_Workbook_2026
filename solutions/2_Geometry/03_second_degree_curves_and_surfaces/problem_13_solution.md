# Exercise 13 — Intersection of a Sphere and a Plane

## Question

Find the intersection curve and radius of \(x^2+y^2+z^2=25\) with \(z=3\). Describe the sections \(z=c\) as \(c\) varies.

## Solution

Substitute \(z=3\):
\[
x^2+y^2+9=25\quad\Longrightarrow\quad x^2+y^2=16.
\]
The intersection is a circle centered at \((0,0,3)\) with radius \(4\). In general, at \(z=c\),
\[
x^2+y^2=25-c^2.
\]
This is a circle of radius \(\sqrt{25-c^2}\) for \(|c|<5\), a single point for \(c=\pm5\), and empty for \(|c|>5\).
