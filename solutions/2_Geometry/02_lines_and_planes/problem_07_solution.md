# Exercise 7 — A Line Relative to a Plane

## Question

For \(L:(x,y,z)=(1,1,0)+t(1,-1,0)\) and \(\Pi:x+y+z=2\), determine whether the line intersects the plane once, is parallel to it, or lies in it. Explain the direction-normal dot product test.

## Solution

The plane has normal \(n=(1,1,1)\), and the line direction is \(v=(1,-1,0)\). Since
\[
v\cdot n=1-1+0=0,
\]
the direction is parallel to the plane. The line's base point gives \(x+y+z=1+1+0=2\), so it lies in the plane. A zero direction-normal dot product means parallel or contained; checking one point distinguishes those cases.
