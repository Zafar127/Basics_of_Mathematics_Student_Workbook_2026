# Exercise 3 — A Square Root and Conjugation

## Solution

Rationalize:
\[
\sqrt{n^2+5n}-n
=\frac{(n^2+5n)-n^2}{\sqrt{n^2+5n}+n}
=\frac{5}{\sqrt{1+5/n}+1}\longrightarrow\frac52.
\]
The original expression subtracts two quantities that both grow like \(n\); multiplying by the conjugate removes that cancellation and gives a quotient with a finite, directly evaluable limit.
