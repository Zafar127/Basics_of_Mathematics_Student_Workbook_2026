# Exercise 8 — Linear Combination and Checking the Result

## Question

Determine whether \(w=(5,1)\) can be written as \(w=au+bv\), where \(u=(1,1)\) and \(v=(2,-1)\). If so, find \(a,b\), reconstruct \(w\), and explain the geometric meaning.

## Solution

Equating components gives
\[
a+2b=5,\qquad a-b=1.
\]
From the second equation \(a=1+b\); substitution gives \(1+3b=5\), so \(b=\frac43\) and \(a=\frac73\). Then
\[
\frac73u+\frac43v
=\left(\frac73+\frac83,\frac73-\frac43\right)=(5,1)=w.
\]
The vectors \(u\) and \(v\) are not parallel, so they span the plane; \(a,b\) are the coordinates of \(w\) in these two directions.
